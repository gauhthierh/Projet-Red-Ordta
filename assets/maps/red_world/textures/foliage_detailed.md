# Feuillage des conifères

Texture `foliage_detailed.png` créée avec l'outil imagegen intégré, utilisée
par les matériaux `leaf` et `leaf_light` du générateur et du chargeur Go.

Prompt :

Create a square seamless tileable opaque diffuse texture for pine tree foliage in a detailed pixel art medieval RPG. Dense overlapping small clusters of evergreen needles, forest green and moss green, muted natural colors, subtle short branch fragments completely covered in foliage. Flat evenly lit material swatch filling whole image edge to edge, no tree silhouette, no sky, no ground, no border, no letters, no transparency, no cast shadows. Suitable repeated on conical 3D pine canopies. Save generated asset for project use.
